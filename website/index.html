1Rule 01Step 03 · Design approval

No reason, no rejection.

Approving a design needs no comment. Sending it back takes a written reason for the designer.

On screenAdd a note explaining what to change. A revision request without a reason gives the designer nothing to act on.

2Rule 02Step 07 · Casting

No photo, no casting.

The casting vendor can’t close the step until a photo of the cast piece is uploaded.

On screenA casting photo is required before marking this stage complete

3Rule 03Step 11 · Quality check

No full checklist, no QC pass.

Every inspection point, hallmark included, must be ticked before QC can sign off. A failed piece needs a defect reason.

On screenQC cannot be authorized with 2 unchecked item(s)

4Rule 04Step 13 · Dispatch

No seal, no dispatch.

The seal, a photo of the packed piece and a contents check must all be ticked before a parcel can leave.

On screenComplete the security protocol first — 1 check(s) outstanding

General notes

  1. 1

    Receipt first

    A client’s stone is logged with its declared value, verifier, time and receipt number before work starts, and photos can be attached. The client acknowledges the risk terms.

  2. 2

    A place for everything

    Every stone and gold lot in stock has a vault location, like Vault A, Tray 4, linked to the job that uses it.

  3. 3

    Gold by the gram

    Gold issued, returned and lost to casting and polishing is recorded by weight, against the job and the step.

  4. 4

    Natural or lab-grown

    Every center and side stone must be marked natural or lab-grown before the order can go ahead. Origin changes value, disclosure and certification.

  5. 5

    Signed and fixed

    Each approval is written to the job’s history with a name and time. That history can’t be edited.

  6. 6

    Access you can revoke

    Your records live on the server, not the phone. A lost device or a departing employee is cut off at once, and their past work stays on record.

What unrecorded gold costs you.

Casting and polishing always lose some metal. When nobody records it by weight, the loss hides inside your margin. Move the sliders to see what that unrecorded loss is worth in a year.

At 4,000 g a month with 1% unrecorded loss and gold at $120 a gram, that is 480 g, or $57,600, a year.

General notes

1

Does it run on iPhone?

The shop-floor app runs on Android phones and tablets, and admins work from a web panel. DhanaOS is built in Flutter, so an iPhone version shares the same code and can be added later.

2

What can vendors and retailers see?

A vendor sees only the jobs sent to them, never a quote or an invoice. A retailer sees only their own orders, plus the quotes and invoices you send them. The database enforces both limits.

3

Which currencies does it handle?

USD and INR, stored as exact amounts with no rounding drift. Indian GST invoicing, with HSN codes and the CGST, SGST and IGST split, is built in.

4

What is it built on?

A Flutter app on Supabase, which provides a PostgreSQL database, logins, file storage and live updates.

5

Who owns the code and data?

You do. The code, the database and the documentation are handed over to you.

6

What isn’t included?

An accounting software link or export, counter billing, payroll, a live gold-rate feed, importing old job history, and devices. Each can be quoted separately.

How it works

Fourteen steps. Three phases. One record.

Every step is closed by name and time-stamped. Casting can’t close without a photo, and QC can’t pass without a full checklist. Two steps happen outside your workshop, and those partners see only the jobs you send them.

Outside partner

Any job can go on hold, be canceled or sent back for rework. Every move is logged with who made it and when.

The rules

Four rules. No exceptions.

They’re enforced by the database, not the app screen. No update, workaround or extra tap gets past them, and the person blocked is told exactly what’s missing.

No.RuleWhat it meansWhat the operator sees
01

No photo, no casting.

The casting vendor can’t close the step until a photo of the cast piece is uploaded.

Operator sees: A casting photo is required before marking this stage complete

02

No reason, no rejection.

Approving a design takes one tap. Sending it back takes a written reason for the designer.

Operator sees: Approving locks v2. Sending it back owes the designer a reason.

03

No full checklist, no QC pass.

Every inspection point, hallmark included, must be ticked before QC can sign off. A failed piece needs a defect reason.

Operator sees: QC cannot be authorized with 2 unchecked item(s)

04

No seal, no dispatch.

The seal, a photo of the packed piece and a contents check must all be ticked before a parcel can leave.

Operator sees: Complete the security protocol first — 1 check(s) outstanding

Custody

Your client’s diamond, on record.

Client-supplied stones and workshop gold are tracked from the moment they arrive until the finished piece ships.

  • Receipt first

    A client’s stone is logged with its declared value, verifier, time and receipt number before work starts, with photos attached. The client acknowledges the risk terms.

  • A place for everything

    Every stone and gold lot has a vault location, like Vault A, Tray 4, linked to the job that uses it.

  • Gold by the gram

    Gold issued, returned and lost to casting and polishing is recorded by weight, against the job and the step.

  • Natural or lab-grown

    Every stone on an order must be marked natural or lab-grown before the order can go ahead. Origin changes value, disclosure and certification.

  • Signed and fixed

    Each approval is written to the job’s history with a name and time. That history can’t be edited.

  • Access you can revoke

    Your records live on the server, not the phone. A lost device or a departing employee is cut off at once, and their past work stays on record.

Access

Partners see only their jobs.

Your team works from the screens for their role. Outside partners are limited by the database itself, so a vendor can’t see another vendor’s jobs, even with a modified app, and never sees your quotes or invoices.

Where each role starts

  • Owner / manager

    Everything, plus what needs a decision today.

  • Front desk

    Orders they open and the customer list.

  • CAD designer

    Jobs assigned to them.

  • Bench, setter, polisher

    Jobs at their step.

  • QC inspector

    Jobs waiting for inspection.

  • Dispatch

    Jobs ready to ship.

  • Vault keeper

    Gold and stone stock.

  • Casting vendor, gem lab Outside

    Only the jobs sent to them. No quotes or invoices.

  • Retailer Outside

    Only their own orders, approvals and invoices.

In the app

Everything a custom order touches.

One app for the shop floor, two portals for the people outside it, and a web panel for whoever runs the office.

Shop floorIn the app

  • Order intake

    A guided form for new designs, remakes, stone resets and repairs. Ring sizes in US, UK and Indian scales.

  • CAD review

    Every model version is kept. Approve it, or send it back with comments.

    Every active job by step. It updates itself as work is closed.

PartnersPortals

AdminIn a browser

FAQ

Straight answers.

Something else? Write to info@madvision.tech.

Does it run on iPhone?

The shop-floor app runs on Android phones and tablets, and admins work from a web panel. DhanaOS is built in Flutter, so an iPhone version shares the same code and can be added later.

What can vendors and retailers see?

A vendor sees only the jobs sent to them, never a quote or an invoice. A retailer sees only their own orders, plus the quotes and invoices you send them. The database enforces both limits.

Which currencies does it handle?

USD and INR, stored as exact amounts with no rounding drift. Indian GST invoicing, with HSN codes and the CGST, SGST and IGST split, is built in.

What is it built on?

A Flutter app on Supabase, which provides a PostgreSQL database, logins, file storage and live updates.

Who owns the code and data?

You do. The code, the database and the documentation are handed over to you.

What isn’t included?

An accounting software link (export is available), counter billing, payroll, a live gold-rate feed, importing old job history, and devices. Each can be quoted separately.

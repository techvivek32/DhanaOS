Controls

Four rules no one can skip.

They're enforced by the database itself. No screen, app update or extra tap can get around them, and the person blocked is told exactly what's missing.

No photo, no casting

The casting vendor can't close the step until they upload a photo of the cast piece.

On screen

A casting photo is required before marking this stage complete

No reason, no rejection

A design can be approved in one tap. Sending it back requires a written comment for the designer.

On screen

Approving locks v2. Sending it back owes the designer a reason.

No full checklist, no QC pass

Every inspection point, hallmark included, must be checked before QC can sign off. A failed piece needs a defect reason.

On screen

QC cannot be authorized with 2 unchecked item(s)

No seal, no dispatch

Seal, paperwork and parcel photos must be complete before a shipment can leave the building.

On screen

Complete the security protocol first — 1 check(s) outstanding

Custody

Your client's diamond, accounted for.

Customer-supplied stones and workshop gold are tracked from the moment they arrive until the finished piece ships.

Receipt before work starts
A customer's stone is logged with its declared value, who verified it, when, a receipt number and photos. The customer acknowledges the risk terms.
A place for everything
Every stone and gold lot has a vault location, like Vault A, Tray 4, and is linked to the job that uses it.
Gold by the gram
Gold issued, returned and lost in casting and polishing is recorded at each step.
Natural or lab-grown, always
Every stone records its origin and the field can't be left blank. Origin changes value, disclosure and certification.
Approvals that stay put
Each approval is saved with a name and time. Nobody can edit it afterward.
Access you can revoke
Nothing sensitive is stored on the phone. A lost device or a departing employee is cut off at once, and their past work stays on record.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Does it run on iPhone?
The shop-floor app runs on Android phones and tablets, and admins work from a web panel. DhanaOS is built in Flutter, so an iPhone version shares the same code and can be added later.
Can vendors or customers see our costs?
No. A vendor sees only the jobs sent to them. A customer sees only their own orders and the prices you send them. The database enforces this, so no app screen can expose it.
Which currencies does it handle?
USD and INR, stored as exact amounts with no rounding drift. Indian GST invoicing, with HSN codes and the CGST, SGST and IGST split, is built in.
What is it built on?
A Flutter app on Supabase, which provides a PostgreSQL database, logins, file storage and live updates.
Who owns the code and data?
You do. The code, the database and the documentation are handed over to you.
What isn't included?
An accounting software link (export is available), counter billing, payroll, a live gold-rate feed, importing old job history, and devices. Each can be quoted separately.
